This piece resembles an abstract physical painting, but does not hide the hard edges and polygons that show its digital origins.

The title of this piece comes from "I'm in the world" by Moondog.

Libraries: p5.js

This is a program, not a picture: each load draws a new variation from a random seed. Originally minted on fxhash. The code here is byte-identical to the original on IPFS (QmbjTJLFHtbri9hxy7yQi9oM8L3LkzP68rU9Ft6Rd3b2Ld).
