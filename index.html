John Rising

I'm an aerospace engineer who builds complex systems and the teams that build them.

I started my career at Virgin Galactic, designing propulsion systems for SpaceShipTwo. I was employee #1 at Relativity Space, where I led vehicle engineering, and I managed missions at SpaceX. I have a master's from MIT, where I studied launch vehicle safety with Nancy Leveson, and an engineering degree from USC.

In 2021 I became a software CEO. I co-founded Stackup, then bymorning.

I live in Los Angeles.
